Tema Chamber of Commerce

Individual Project Site Plan — WDD 231

Site Name

Tema Chamber of Commerce

This name was selected because it clearly identifies the organization, its geographic focus on Tema, Ghana, and its mission to support local business development. The title is professional, memorable, and suitable for branding across the website and promotional materials.

Optional domain: temachamber.com

Site Purpose

The Tema Chamber of Commerce website serves as a central hub for businesses, residents, and visitors in the Tema area. The site provides a member business directory, community discovery resources, local weather updates, membership information, and an online application form. Its purpose is to strengthen the local economy by helping businesses connect, promoting chamber events, and encouraging community engagement.

Scenarios

  • A small business owner in Tema wants to join the chamber to network with other entrepreneurs. They visit the site to compare membership levels, review benefits, and complete the online application form.
  • A resident new to Tema is looking for trusted local services such as printing, construction, or food suppliers. They use the business directory to search member companies and find contact details.
  • A community member planning their week wants to know what is happening locally and whether outdoor events will have good weather. They check the home page for current conditions and browse featured business spotlights.
  • An investor researching Tema's business climate visits the Discover page to learn about the city's industries, demographics, and growth opportunities before contacting the chamber.

Color Scheme

The palette below is applied throughout this site plan document and will carry into the final project website.

  • Primary — #004b87 (Dark Blue)

    Used for the header, footer, main headings, and primary navigation to convey trust and professionalism.

  • Secondary — #006bb3 (Medium Blue)

    Used for subheadings, links, wireframe borders, and hover states on interactive elements.

  • Accent — #f2c94c (Gold)

    Used for section dividers, call-to-action highlights, and decorative borders to add energy and draw attention.

  • Background — #ffffff (White)

    Used for the page background and content areas to keep text readable and the layout clean.

  • Text — #333333 (Dark Gray)

    Used for body copy and list content to maintain strong contrast against light backgrounds.

Typography

Three complementary Google Fonts are selected to keep the design consistent and readable.

  • Poppins (700) — Headings (h1, h2, h3) and the site name for a bold, modern look.
  • Poppins (400) — Body text, paragraphs, and list items for comfortable reading.
  • Roboto (500) — Navigation labels and button text for clarity at smaller sizes.
  • Roboto Mono (500) — Wireframe labels and the optional domain display for a technical, blueprint feel.
